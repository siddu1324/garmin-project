What are we trying to predict?

The study’s three-item perceived-stress composite combines nervousness with reverse-coded coping and control. The stored item codes are integers from 1 to 5, even though the questionnaire displays anchors from 0 to 4.

PSS composite = nervous + (6 − cope) + (6 − control)
TaskDefinitionInterpretation
Primary: three classes0: 3–4 · 1: 5–9 · 2: 10–15Low / middle / high within this study
Secondary: binaryPSS ≥5; separately, PSS ≥10Two exploratory questions, evaluated at 60 minutes

There are 3,689 valid scores and 17 incomplete scores. An incomplete response stays in the data with a missing outcome; it is never assigned a class or imputed.

Exploratory categories, not clinical diagnoses

This is neither PSS-10 nor PHQ-9. “High” means a composite of 10–15 under the chosen research definition, not a diagnostic threshold. The report audits the development pipeline; it does not re-adjudicate postpartum eligibility.

METHODS REVIEW / 02 OF 10

The pipeline preserves evidence before it summarizes it.

Raw records are retained. Analysis views and model inputs are separate, traceable layers.

  1. Read corrected exportsGarmin ZIP/CSV streams + Morning, Midday and Endday EMA tables.
  2. Preserve & normalizeKeep raw strings, hashes and row numbers; add parsed values and clock flags.
  3. Verify identityUse exact participant keys, an unambiguous crosswalk and source-hash corroboration.
  4. Set the cutoffUse actual EMA start, its row’s offset and its precision-specific buffer.
  5. Query each lookbackSelect that participant’s timestamp groups; retain acceptance and rejection ledgers.
  6. Calculate featuresObserved HR summaries and supported step additions; missing remains explicit.
  7. Join the outcomeOne-to-one on stable EMA key; assert participant, category, clock and fold consistency.
  8. Evaluate by participantTrain transformations and logistic regression inside grouped folds; score unseen participants.

Physical record ≠ analysis reading

The normalized development bundle preserves 57,552,038 physical sensor rows. Its logical view contains 57,551,189 after marking 849 exact-duplicate excess rows. Both views retain provenance.

One participant’s data stay together

Frozen assignments determine who is development-only and which fold each response belongs to. Missing Garmin history does not remove an EMA from the master table.

Identity is checked, not guessed

A respondent ID is not always unique. A direct participant key must not contradict a usable respondent crosswalk. Keyless sources require an exact source-content hash matched to the previously verified registry; filename resemblance alone is insufficient.

Implementation & evidence

garmin_inventory/audit.py::resolve_pair; garmin_preprocessing/normalize.py::reference_identity_registry, normalize_chunk, normalize_ema, annotate_duplicates. Chunks contain 100,000 rows; participant/sensor partitions have a configured 3,000,000-row safety ceiling. Original fields and source schemas survive Parquet round-trip checks. The stable n_ema_id encodes source-table index and original source row, not an inferred scheduled event. link_outcomes verifies all join metadata.

METHODS REVIEW / 03 OF 10

Align to actual start—not the scheduled prompt.

Every wearable lookback is left-inclusive and right-exclusive: [cutoff − L, cutoff).

EMA clock

UTC = actual local start − row offset

The offset is local minus UTC, in minutes. Synthetic example: 09:22 with offset −300 becomes 14:22 UTC, then a minute-resolution cutoff of 14:21 UTC.

Garmin clock

UTC = Unix seconds in ts

No second timezone shift is applied. dte_tme is used to flag clock disagreement and identify local-day transitions, not to move data into better alignment.

HR/activity uses one direct lookback for each duration. It does not average five-minute IBI windows. The IBI audit’s five-minute windows, one-minute steps and interval-start/end hypotheses are a separate branch.

METHODS REVIEW / 04 OF 10

HR acceptance is deliberately simple—and provisional.

A plausible reading is not automatically a high-quality measurement or a representative window.

ClockFinite valid Unix epoch; parseable text clock; no discrepancy or unusual-offset flag.
ValueFinite HR in 30–240 bpm, inclusive. Zero, missing and nonnumeric values are rejected.
TiesSame timestamp and value contributes once only if every member passes the clock/value checks.
ConflictDifferent values at the same timestamp invalidate the whole group. No averaging.
RepeatsEqual HR values at different timestamps remain separate observations.
What these checks do not establish

No verified device-quality flag, rhythm label or contact/motion confidence is available in the six-field sensor export. The range check is an engineering screen, not physiological validation.

There is no HR maximum-gap or freshness requirement in the primary cohort. Gaps are recorded separately; no missing readings are interpolated.

METHODS REVIEW / 05 OF 10

From accepted readings to physiological predictors.

The same formulas are applied separately to each lookback. Source values and quality diagnostics remain separate.

HR mean · bpm

μ = Σvᵢ / n

Arithmetic mean of accepted HR values. Source: val → n_value; each direct lookback L.

Support / missing: ≥2 distinct accepted timestamps and span ≥1 s; otherwise missing.

Interpretation: Each reading receives equal weight. Dense bursts contribute more than sparsely sampled minutes.

HR standard deviation · bpm

s = √[Σ(vᵢ − μ)² / (n − 1)]

Sample SD of those same accepted values. This is variation in observed HR, not beat-to-beat HRV.

Support / missing: Same ≥2 timestamps / ≥1 s gate as mean; otherwise missing.

Interpretation: Variation may reflect activity, measurement error or changing coverage as well as physiology.

Latest HR · bpm

latest = v at the greatest t < cutoff

The last accepted value inside that direct lookback. Source: n_epoch_s, n_value.

Support / missing: ≥1 accepted reading; otherwise missing. No reading from outside the interval is carried forward.

Interpretation: “Latest” does not mean recent. Freshness is a separate diagnostic, not a primary eligibility gate.

HR slope · bpm per minute

β = Σ[(xᵢ − x̄)(vᵢ − μ)] / Σ(xᵢ − x̄)²

Here xᵢ = (tᵢ − t₁)/60. Ordinary least squares with an intercept, using actual elapsed minutes.

Support / missing: ≥3 distinct accepted timestamps spanning ≥60 s; otherwise missing.

Interpretation: A line through irregular observations can span long gaps; it is not a continuously observed trend.

Activity and context

Supported steps · count

steps = Σ(cᵢ − cᵢ₋₁) over eligible pairs

Source: data_type=steps, val → n_value, Unix times, local date, offset and source hash. Each L.

Support / missing: At least one pair of original consecutive timestamp groups; both endpoints accepted and inside the window; 0 < Δt ≤300 s; same local day, offset and source; nonnegative integer counters and nonnegative change. Otherwise missing.

Interpretation: A partial supported sum, not total activity for the window. Never prorate a boundary-crossing increment or bridge a rejected record.

Local time sine and cosine

sin(2πu / 86400), cos(2πu / 86400)

u is seconds since local midnight at the actual EMA start, including subsecond precision if present. Source: n_actual_local. Same context across lookbacks.

Support / missing: Requires parseable actual local time and valid context. Invalid context excludes the response from fitting, without deleting the master row.

Interpretation: Encodes a cyclic clock, not time since waking or since the last prompt.

EMA survey category · context, not physiology

Morning, Midday or Endday is derived from the source survey schema: presence of SLEEP_SLEEPTIME → Morning; ENDDAY_TEXT → Endday; otherwise Midday. No answered item value is used to assign category.

one-hot(category) = [Endday, Midday, Morning]

Known valid category is required for the model cohort. The encoder is learned in training, keeps all categories, and maps unseen validation categories to all zeros. Category is constant across a response’s lookbacks; it does not prove the response’s exact recall interval.

SEPARATE EXPERIMENT

Earlier-recording HR reference and deviations

For a current 60-minute window, use only [cutoff −25 h, cutoff −1 h). First cut the readings to that interval; then average readings within each occupied UTC minute. Empty minutes remain missing.

Source: the accepted HR ledger’s n_epoch_s, n_value and a_analysis_keep; deviations also use the current 60-minute mean/latest columns.

reference = (1/M) Σ occupied-minute means

Require ≥30 occupied UTC-minute bins across ≥3 UTC-hour bins. Otherwise reference and dependent deviations are missing. It is an earlier-recording reference—not resting HR or a complete daily baseline.

mean deviation = current HR mean − reference

Needs both the current mean and reference.

latest deviation = current latest HR − reference

Needs both the current latest and reference.

Important asymmetry: the current mean weights readings equally; the reference weights occupied minutes equally. A deviation can therefore change with sampling density, not only physiology. Held-out participants contribute only their earlier unlabeled wearable history.

What exactly enters logistic regression?

The model receives numeric summaries and encoded context—not sensor streams, identities or survey answers.

ComparisonInputs at one lookbackEncoded dimensions¹
Context onlyLocal time sine + cosine; survey category5
Context + HRContext + HR mean, SD, latest, slope9
Context + HR + stepsAbove + supported step additions10

¹ With all three survey categories present: 3 one-hot columns plus the numeric features. The fitted intercept is additional. All comparisons within a cohort use identical training and validation membership.

Training-only transformation

Standardize each numeric feature as z = (x − training mean) / training SD; the scaler uses population SD (N), unlike the HR feature’s sample SD (N−1). A constant training column uses unit scale.

One-hot encode survey category using training categories; keep all categories. The encoder and scaler are refitted in every inner and outer training split.

Logistic specification

L2 regularization; lbfgs; at most 2,000 iterations. Choose C ∈ {0.01, 0.1, 1, 10} by inner-fold mean macro F1. Three classes use multinomial probabilities; binary tasks use the corresponding two-class model.

Unweighted is the primary result. A separate balanced sensitivity uses training weights n/(K × class count). Predict by largest probability; no validation-based cutoff search.

One synthetic model input, end to end

For the worked Morning EMA: mean=90, SD=30, latest=120, slope=30, steps=24. Actual local start 09:22 gives sine=0.636, cosine=-0.772. Nervous=4, cope=2, control=3 gives PSS=11 → class 2.

Using a separate, explicitly synthetic training set, the actual transformer produces:

[0.900, -0.445, 0.000, 1.225, 0.816, 1.225, 1.225, 0.000, 0.000, 1.000]

Order: standardized sine, cosine, mean, SD, latest, slope, steps; then Endday, Midday, Morning. The last three values are [0,0,1]. The target is separate and never appears in this vector. No real participant row or fitted real-data statistic is used here.

Keep people—not individual responses—apart

The original five folds each contain 25 development participants. For each outer fold: hold its participants out; run three grouped inner folds only among the remaining eligible participants; choose C; refit transformations and model on outer training responses; predict the untouched outer fold.

Every eligible response gets one held-out prediction. A participant’s repeated responses never cross a training/validation boundary. Overlapping sensor windows are not independent observations.

Exact vector names and imputed/history variants

local_time_sin → local_time_cos → hr_mean_bpm__60m → hr_sd_bpm__60m → hr_latest_bpm__60m → hr_slope_bpm_per_minute__60m → steps_added_supported__60m → n_survey_category_Endday → n_survey_category_Midday → n_survey_category_Morning

In the imputed branch, training-median filling precedes standardization; separate 0/1 missingness flags are appended without standardization. Only permitted wearable features receive flags. A context-plus-missingness-only control excludes their values. Historical reference/deviation columns are added only in the separately matched 60-minute history experiment. Quality counts, sensor stress, HRV, participant ID, fold ID and current answers are never primary predictors.

AUROC is the macro average of three one-versus-rest probability rankings, pooled over held-out predictions. Balanced accuracy averages the three recalls. Logistic’s balanced accuracy is near one-third; its AUROC is below 0.50.

The high class is not detected

The context + HR + steps logistic model predicts no class-2 responses at its default decision rule: high-class recall and precision are both 0. Its small macro-F1 increase over majority does not establish useful physiological discrimination.

METHODS REVIEW / 10 OF 10

A focused discussion with Will.

Improve the measurement and experimental comparison before increasing model complexity.

1

Resolve source semantics first

Will: can we obtain the collection/exporter version, timestamp assignment code, ordered original payloads, prompt delivery logs and sensor-quality fields? These determine which temporal claims are defensible.

2

Test the weighting of observations

Compare reading-weighted HR means with equal-weight means of observed minutes. Leave empty minutes missing; use identical responses and unchanged folds. This is not continuous time weighting.

3

Prespecify support and missingness comparisons

Choose a small, justified support set before fitting; compare unchanged predictions on subsets separately from retraining. Pair complete-case, train-only imputed and missingness-only models on matched validation responses. Earlier support experiments already exist; reuse their findings rather than rerun blindly.

4

Align the question and the interval

Use documented prompt/wake boundaries where available. Label current-response wake windows retrospective. Do not substitute the previous completed survey for the previous prompt or invent waking from Garmin records.

5

Keep the next model simple

Use logistic regression for a small prespecified feature-block ablation: context → HR → supported activity, and a separate history test. Any selection must occur inside training folds; do not select a window from the best observed result. Assess high-class recall/precision alongside macro F1 and AUROC.

The decision to make

Which measurement assumption can we verify or test next, with a clear expected effect on the inputs? Agree on that question, freeze the comparison, and retain the same participant-grouped evaluation. There is no automatic new model search attached to this report.
